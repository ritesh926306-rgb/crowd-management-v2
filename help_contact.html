Lost & Found Protocol

If you have lost personal belongings or need assistance locating accompanying family members:

  • Visit the Central Information Desk near the main plaza.
  • Present your official Registration Code so staff can verify your identity and group records.
  • Unclaimed items are safely logged and stored in the security office.
🚑

First Aid & Paramedic Stations

Full medical teams and ambulances are on standby throughout the venue grounds:

  • Station Alpha: Located adjacent to Exit A (West Gate).
  • Station Beta: Positioned at Exit B (North Corridor).
  • Station Gamma: Located near East Plaza (Exit C).
  • Water stations and basic medical kits are stationed at every exit corridor.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I register my entire family under one pass?

Yes. On Step 3 of registration, click "+ Add Family / Group Member" to include companions. You will receive one master digital pass with the verified group headcount.

Q: How do I show my pass at the gate?

Simply present the QR code on your smartphone screen to the security guard at Gate 1. You may also present a clear printed copy.

Q: What should I do if my phone battery dies?

Visit the Visitor Help Desk at Gate 1 with your registered 10-digit mobile number and government photo ID. On-duty staff can look up and print your verified pass.

Q: Why is a specific exit gate assigned to me?

The system balances crowd distribution across Exit Corridors A, B, C, and D to prevent bottlenecks and ensure a safe, comfortable departure for all attendees.

🛡️ Privacy & Data Protection

VenueGuard collects visitor name, age, mobile number, and address strictly for authorized venue entry verification, emergency communication, and event safety management.

Your data is cryptographically protected and never sold or shared with commercial third parties. Digital passes mask personal phone numbers and private addresses on public views.

📋 Venue Entry Terms & Conditions

  • Each digital pass is valid only for the registered group size and assigned date.
  • Passes must be presented to official security guards at designated entry points.
  • Pass duplication or unauthorized sharing is strictly prohibited under anti-passback security protocols.
  • Visitors must adhere to assigned exit routes and follow safety directions issued by event security.
