🚗 Parking & Transit Hubs

🅿️ North Open Parking (Lot A) Directly accessible from Exit B (North). Features 400 vehicle capacity and designated two-wheeler bays.
🚇 Metro & City Shuttle Service Feeder shuttles depart every 10 minutes from East Plaza immediately outside Exit C (East).
🚕 Taxi & Rideshare Pickup Zone Dedicated rideshare drop-off and pickup stands are located along South Promenade near Exit D.

📋 Visitor Guidelines & Code of Conduct

  • Please arrive 15 minutes before your preferred entry window to avoid checkpoint queues.
  • Carry your digital pass on a mobile phone or printed paper pass with clear QR visibility.
  • Do not share QR screenshots with unauthorized persons — all passes are authenticated upon gate entry.
  • Follow directional signage and maintain orderly movement through assigned corridors.
  • For safety, luggage and large backpacks are subject to security screening at Gate 1.

Privacy Policy • Terms of Entry

🛡️ Privacy & Data Protection

VenueGuard collects visitor name, age, mobile number, and address strictly for authorized venue entry verification, emergency communication, and event safety management.

Your data is cryptographically protected and never sold or shared with commercial third parties. Digital passes mask personal phone numbers and private addresses on public views.

📋 Venue Entry Terms & Conditions

  • Each digital pass is valid only for the registered group size and assigned date.
  • Passes must be presented to official security guards at designated entry points.
  • Pass duplication or unauthorized sharing is strictly prohibited under anti-passback security protocols.
  • Visitors must adhere to assigned exit routes and follow safety directions issued by event security.
